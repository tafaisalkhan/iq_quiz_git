Privacy Policy for IQ Quiz

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

IQ Quiz ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application IQ Quiz (Package Name: com.iqquiz.app).

1. Information Collection and Use

We prioritize user privacy and data security. IQ Quiz functions as a standalone brain training and cognitive testing app:

  • Personal Data: We do NOT collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • App Storage & Data: Your quiz scores, scheduled test preferences, timer preferences, and unlocked sessions are stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage (SharedPreferences).

2. Third-Party Advertising & Services

IQ Quiz uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Ads) to keep the application free for users:

  • Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, and diagnostic data to serve non-personalized or personalized ads.
  • For more information on how Google collects and uses data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Users have the option to purchase "Remove Ads" via Google Play In-App Billing. All financial transactions are processed securely through Google Play Store services. We do not store or process payment or credit card details.

4. Permissions Used

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver daily quiz reminders and scheduled test alerts to your Android status bar.
  • Vibration & Audio: Used exclusively for in-app haptic feedback on wrong answers and celebratory applause sounds when achieving high scores.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to trigger native scheduled quiz alerts.

5. Children's Privacy

IQ Quiz does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.
